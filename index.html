FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EQUIPMENT & FREIGHT SUPPORT

1. How are heavy engines and power tillers packaged for freight? ▼
All equipment is secured to reinforced heat-treated wooden pallets, shrink-wrapped in moisture-barrier plastic, padded with shock-absorbing foam, and encased in a solid timber export crate. All shipments carry full transit insurance.
2. Does the engine arrive pre-serviced with oil and accessories? ▼
Yes! Every engine shipment includes a complimentary bottle of high-grade diesel engine oil, an emergency mechanic toolkit (spanners, socket wrench, injector maintenance tool), decompression crank handle, fueling funnel, and factory service manuals.
3. Can I convert a manual hand-crank engine to one-touch electric start? ▼
Yes! We supply a complete 1-Touch 12V Electric Starter Upgrade Kit comprising a high-torque reduction starter motor, hardened ring gear, mounting bracket, key ignition switch panel, and wiring harness. Installation takes approximately 30 minutes with standard hand tools.
4. What is your warranty coverage and technical support process? ▼
All commercial diesel engines carry an official 24-month manufacturer warranty. Our technical engineering team is available 7 days a week via WhatsApp video consultation at +84 918 453 476 to assist with installation, pulley sizing, or immediate spare parts dispatch.
