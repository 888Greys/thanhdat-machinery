FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EQUIPMENT & FREIGHT SUPPORT

1. How are heavy engines and power tillers packaged for freight? ▼
All equipment is secured to reinforced heat-treated wooden pallets, shrink-wrapped in moisture-barrier plastic, padded with shock-absorbing foam, and encased in a solid timber export crate. All shipments carry full transit insurance.
2. Do machines arrive pre-serviced and ready to run? ▼
Petrol and diesel machines are pre-serviced and run-tested before dispatch, and ship with a basic tool kit, fueling funnel and service manual. Electric machines such as the milking unit and pellet press arrive ready to connect to mains power.
3. Do the walk-behind tractors come with implements included? ▼
Yes. The JIADI walking tractors ship complete with disc ploughs, arrow ploughs, rotavators and iron traction wheels, plus both rubber tyres and iron wheels for road and field work. Tell us what you are planting and we will confirm the right implement set.
4. What is your warranty coverage and technical support process? ▼
Equipment carries an official 24-month manufacturer warranty, backed by our workshop's spare parts stock. Our technical team is available 7 days a week via WhatsApp video consultation at +254 795 365 461 to assist with setup, implement selection, or immediate spare parts dispatch.
