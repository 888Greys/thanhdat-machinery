FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EQUIPMENT & ORDERING SUPPORT

1. How are heavy engines and tillers packaged for freight delivery? ▼
All equipment is secured onto heat-treated wooden pallets, shrink-wrapped in protective plastic moisture barrier, padded with shock-absorbing foam, and enclosed in a heavy timber crate. Crated units are fully insured and shipped via established freight carriers.
2. Does the engine arrive pre-serviced with oil and accessories? ▼
Yes! Each engine purchase includes a complimentary container of premium diesel motor oil, an emergency toolkit (spark plug/injector spanners and adjustment wrenches), manual decompression hand crank, fueling funnel, and factory operation manuals.
3. Can I upgrade my manual hand-crank engine to electric key start? ▼
Absolutely! We supply a complete 1-Touch 12V Electric Starter Upgrade Kit comprising a high-torque reduction starter, hardened ring gear, mounting bracket, and ignition key panel. Installation takes approximately 30 minutes with basic shop tools.
4. What is your warranty coverage and technical support process? ▼
All diesel engines carry a 24-month comprehensive manufacturer warranty. Our technical team is available 7 days a week via WhatsApp video consultation at +84 918 453 476 to assist with installation, governor tuning, or immediate spare parts dispatch.
